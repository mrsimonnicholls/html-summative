Summative 01:
New Enterprise

BMD2ID2: Interactive Design Major Studio 2

Level 6, 40 Credits

Assessment Weighting: 40%

Brief

It's the year 2027. As a recent graduate of Media Design School, you decide to put your design skills toward setting up a new social and/or commercial enterprise in Auckland, New Zealand.

Demonstrate the important contribution design can make to social, cultural and community wellbeing, quality of life and identity. Use a lean, human-centred design approach to determine the concept and scope of the enterprise, and the interactive product or service that this enterprise offers.

Areas of inquiry to consider: improved social equity; civic participation and innovation; environmental regeneration; healthy communities; housing affordability; food security; arts and cultural expression. Your designed solution must relate to an identified present day, real world problem.

You will need to consider an appropriate target market carefully so that you can tailor your solution, test appropriately and promote your enterprise across relevant media channels.

You will need to develop a creative media strategy to launch your product or service. Digital mockups as well as MVP digital product are expected as part of a final presentation.

Suggested timeline

Week 01

UX Research

Produce a problem statement for identified and researched problem.

Week 02

WIP 01

Prepare a work in progress presentation according to the supplied specifications.

Week 03

Lofi Prototype & Testing

Test protypes and proof of concept for your product/marketing site.

Week 04

Pitch Presentation

Build moodboards, stylescapes and a pitch to sell your idea.

Week 05

Prototype Identity

Develop the brand for your solution. Build brand guidelines & styleguide.

Week 06

Hifi Prototype & WIP 02

Build a hifi prototype then showcase and get feedback during WIP 02.

Week 07

Testing & Production

Test then build your hifi prototype using HTML, CSS, JS & a database.

Week 08

Submission

Deploy your MVP. Submit all collateral & case study to Canvas.
